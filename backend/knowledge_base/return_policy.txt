Retail Return Policy

Section 1: General Returns
- Standard returns are allowed for items returned in original condition within 30 days of delivery.
- Damaged items must be reported within 7 days of delivery and may require supporting evidence.
- Returns outside the original return window may be denied unless a policy exception is approved.

Section 2: Policy Exceptions
- High-value electronics returned as damaged may require specialist review if the value exceeds $1,000 or there are unusual damage patterns.
- If a customer has repeated prior returns or elevated account risk, a fraud review may be triggered.

Section 3: Fraud Review
- Repeated return claims with mismatched evidence or suspicious patterns are routed to fraud review.
- A fraud review requires human approval before any adjustment or refund is finalized.
